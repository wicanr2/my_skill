## Base template

```text
Instrumental [use and length]. [World/year/hardware identity]. Player state: [action and emotional
arc]. Motif: [3–7 notes/rhythm], transformed by [state]. [Instrument] does [behavior and entry];
[instrument] does [behavior and entry]. Form: [sections/loop/timecodes]. Deliver [master, stems,
stingers, MIDI]. Avoid [project-specific clichés]. Label as [original/arrangement/new remake cue].
```

## Exploration

```text
Instrumental seamless exploration loop, [90] seconds. A [historical identity] RPG world defined
by [three materials]. Patient discovery with distant danger, never urgent. [Lead instrument]
states [motif] once every [seconds]; [bass] sustains open fifths; [cultural/environment instrument]
answers sparsely. Percussion enters only as a faint travel pulse after the midpoint. Deliver
ambience and harmony stems plus a reverb-safe tail. Avoid heroic cadences, constant ostinato,
choir, EDM, and recognizable themes.
```

## Town

```text
Instrumental [port/border/desert/underground] town loop, [60–90] seconds. The settlement feels
[safe but poor/busy but threatened/sacred but corrupt]. [Two small-ensemble instruments] trade
short phrases over [rhythm instrument] at [BPM]. Keep dialogue and menu sounds clear. Eight-bar
A, contrasting B, return without final cadence. No tavern cliché unless the location is actually
a tavern; no large orchestra, choir, or trailer drums.
```

## Dungeon

```text
Dark dungeon ambience-music hybrid, seamless [90] seconds. The space is made of [materials]; the
danger is [religious/undead/mechanical/demonic]. [Low instrument] makes irregular breath-like
swells; isolated [percussion material] has long non-rhythmic decay; fragments of [motif] omit the
final note. Preserve silence and use at most [layer count] simultaneous layers. No steady combat
beat, periodic jump-scare riser, or generic horror drone.
```

## Combat

```text
Layered turn-based RPG combat cue, [96] BPM, [60] second seamless loop. Tactical and decisive,
not frantic. Strings perform a syncopated reduction of [motif]; low drums mark decisions rather
than every beat; brass answers at round boundaries. Deliver synchronized pulse, harmony, threat,
and impact stems. It must survive three repeated loops. Avoid nonstop cymbals, double-time
trailer ostinato, power metal, and permanent maximum intensity.
```

## Boss

```text
Final boss music for [identity and conflict], [duration/loop]. Begin with the player motif in its
familiar instrument, then let the antagonist distort it by [interval/rhythm/orchestration].
Reserve choir and low brass for phase [number]. Create a real [1–3] second void before the
irreversible decision. The climax sounds costly, not automatically victorious. End with
[unresolved/tragic/costly-victory] harmony. No generic Latin syllables, borrowed liturgical text,
constant wall of sound, named-artist imitation, or franchise themes.
```

## Trailer

```text
[60–75] second instrumental RPG remake trailer score synchronized to picture. 0–[A] historical
restraint; [A]–[B] world reveal; [B]–[C] gameplay acceleration; [C]–[D] silence before decision;
[D] climax; final [seconds] unresolved logo tail. Build from [project motif/original SFX rhythm]
transformed across [instruments]. Leave spectral space for dialogue and authentic SFX. Deliver
full mix, music-only, MIDI, and four stems. Target [LUFS], true peak below [-1 dBTP]. Label as new
remake/trailer music, not original soundtrack.
```

## QA

- Can listeners identify the world without a filename?
- Do exploration, danger, and combat differ in more than loudness?
- Is the third loop fatiguing?
- Are dialogue and UI sounds intelligible?
- Are loop boundaries and transition bars clean?
- Do all valid stem combinations remain harmonic and unclipped?
- Are original cues, arrangements, and new music labeled separately?
- Are prompt, seed, model/tier, terms snapshot, source motif, and human edits archived?
