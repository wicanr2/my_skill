# Remake README standard

Use README as a stable, readable, evidence-linked landing page—not a worklog or complete research DB.

Prefer this order: title and one-line purpose; rights/original-assets boundary; game and preservation
value; current playable status and limits; representative verified results; clearly captioned runtime
or comparison images; shortest safe run/import path; links to current status, specs, research, and
releases; license, credits, and non-affiliation statement.

Tone may be documentary, technical, historical, or lightly magazine-like when it fits the game. Do
not force first-person nostalgia or a single house voice. Keep chronological commands, failures,
commits, and checkpoints in `WORKLOG.md`.

Every percentage states its denominator and unknowns. Every parity or playability claim names the
tested state and normal player path, and links evidence. Distinguish research images from runtime
screenshots and never embed protected original assets. Keep one status summary and link its detailed
source of truth rather than maintaining drifting copies.
